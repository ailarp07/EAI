The major

A Mathayom 4 to 6 major at Satit Prasarnmit (Secondary) that builds knowledge and technical ability across computing, automation, robotics and AI, combining theory with hands-on practice in preparation for higher education. See About.

The curriculum emphasizes applied, hands-on learning, from foundational engineering and programming to real-world problem solving with robotics and microcontrollers, including coursework certified by FIBO, KMUTT.

Year 1 (M.4) covers foundations: code, circuits and first robots. Year 2 (M.5) is applied AI: vision, deep learning and single-board computers. Year 3 (M.6) is the capstone: independent research, robotics and the major project. Lesson write-ups are on Classwork Report.

Every term has 40 periods a week, and credits run from 14.0 to 15.5 a semester. Core subjects such as Thai, mathematics, science, social studies and English continue alongside the major subjects.

Yes. Students study electrical circuits and microcontrollers, Physics in Robotics Technology and Microcontrollers & AI, and build robots in class. Some coursework is certified by FIBO, KMUTT.

Graduating and activities

By the first semester of M.6: attend the major camp at least once and help run the open-house presentation, attend at least 2 skill-development activities, enter or compete in a technology contest, join a school-arranged site visit, present a personal project at an exhibition, and submit one English proficiency test result.

You need to submit at least one English proficiency test result (such as IELTS, TOEIC, CU-TEP or TU-GET) by M.6 semester 1. No minimum score is required.

Entering an innovation or invention contest in engineering, computing or technology is one of the graduation requirements. Past results are on the Works page.

Study visits and workshops, 3 to 5 field trips a year, a yearly 3-day 2-night major camp, and robotics and project competitions. See Events.

After graduating

Over 72% of alumni are admitted through TCAS Round 1, most continuing into engineering faculties.

Of 122 alumni, 119 have a placement. The most common are KMITL (34), Chulalongkorn (32), KMUTT (19), Thammasat (14) and Kasetsart (6). See Universities Stats.

AI / ML engineer, computer vision engineer, robotics engineer, embedded engineer or data scientist, among others. The About page maps each career to its subjects.

Yes. Most alumni choose engineering, but some went on to science, business, architecture and psychology.

Life at school

Homeroom is at 07:45. Lunch runs 11:20 to 12:55, and it is best to go early because the lines are already packed by 11:20. Some periods are a flipped classroom, where you watch the lesson as a video. On projects with a lot of hardware you sometimes stay at school until about 18:00 to 19:00. Please talk to your parents beforehand, and expect transport to be crammed at that hour.

The AI computer room is room 1909 on the 9th floor. Food is not allowed in the room.

The school is on Sukhumvit 23, Watthana, Bangkok. Take the BTS to Asok or the MRT to Sukhumvit, bus 38 or 136 to Asok Montri Road, or the Saen Saep canal boat to Asok or Prasarnmit pier. More on Life at EAI.
