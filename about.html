The AI Engineering major: what it teaches and how the three years are laid out.

The major

Mathayom 4–6 at Satit Prasarnmit Demonstration School, Bangkok.

The Artificial Intelligence Engineering major develops students’ knowledge, reasoning, and technical ability across computing, automation, robotics, and artificial intelligence. The curriculum integrates mathematics and science with the design and development of AI systems and software capable of addressing real-world problems, combining theoretical study with applied practice in preparation for higher education.

Major graduation requirements

Every student in the AI Engineering major must clear the major-completion requirements by the first semester of M.6:

01

Attend the major camp at least once across all 3 years, and help run the major's open-house presentation.

02

Attend at least 2 skill-development activities (with proof of attendance).

03

Enter an innovation/invention contest in engineering, computing, or technology - or compete in one of the same fields.

04

Join a school-arranged site visit to a public or private organization related to engineering, computing, or technology.

05

Develop a project of personal interest and present it at an exhibition, in or outside school.

06

Submit at least one English proficiency test result (e.g. IELTS, TOEIC, CU-TEP, TU-GET) to the major's faculty by M.6 semester 1 (no minimum score required).
