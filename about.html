About us

The AI Engineering major: what it teaches and how the three years are laid out.

Artificial Intelligence Engineering

Satit Prasarnmit Demonstration School (Secondary Division)

The Artificial Intelligence Engineering major develops students’ knowledge, reasoning, and technical ability across computing, automation, robotics, and artificial intelligence. The curriculum integrates mathematics and science with the design and development of AI systems and software capable of addressing real-world problems, combining theoretical study with applied practice in preparation for higher education.

Major graduation requirements

Every student must complete all six requirements by the first semester of M.6.

Major camp

Join at least once across the three years, and help run the major's open-house presentation.

Skill-development activities

Take part in at least 2, with proof of attendance.

Contest or competition

Enter an engineering, computing or technology innovation contest, or compete in one of the same fields.

Site visit

Join a school-arranged visit to a public or private organization in engineering, computing or technology.

Project and exhibition

Build a project around your own interest, then present it at an exhibition in or outside school.

English proficiency

Submit at least one standard test result, such as IELTS, TOEIC, CU-TEP or TU-GET, to the major's faculty by M.6 semester 1. No minimum score.
