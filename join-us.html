How to apply

What we look for, how applicants are scored and the rounds to get in.

Requirements for all applicants

No coding exam. Instructors start from the fundamentals.

Cumulative GPA 2.00 or above

Over 5 semesters. Same for internal and external applicants.

Three opportunities for internal students

Students already enrolled in the school’s lower secondary division get three opportunities to enter before competing with external applicants, under the same requirements.

Round 1 · Main round

The primary examination round, open to internal students for all five majors, selecting one as first choice. Applicants meeting the GPA requirement may walk in without prior registration.

Walk-in

Round 2 · Supplementary round

An additional round held when seats remain after the main round. Not offered every year, subject to official school announcement.

Varies by year

Round 3 · External round

Internal students who miss the first two rounds may apply alongside external applicants, under identical requirements and criteria.
